Fun Facts in Anime

Did you know that the longest-running anime series is "Sazae-san," which has been airing since 1969? It holds the Guinness World Record for the longest-running animated television series. Another interesting fact is that the anime industry in Japan is worth billions of dollars and has a massive global fanbase. Additionally, many anime series are adapted from manga, light novels, or video games, showcasing the diverse storytelling mediums in Japanese pop culture.
